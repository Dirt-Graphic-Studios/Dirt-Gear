MX Bikes rider gear editor · Windows

DIRT GEAR

Design custom MX Bikes rider gear from a creator's PSD template. Pick colourways, add your logos, name and number, check it on a 3D rider, and pack it straight into the game. No Photoshop needed.

Download for Windows Fair use

Free to download. Windows 10 or 11. Everything stays on your PC.

What it does

From template to track.

Open any gear PSD

Dirt Gear reads the template's layers and finds the Norm and Spec maps for you. Show or hide layers with one click.

Logos under the shading

Drop in PNG, JPG or SVG logos and place them beneath the template's shading so they sit naturally on the gear.

Names & numbers

Bundled race fonts with outlines, gradients and letter spacing for your rider name and number.

Colour overlays

Recolour any logo with a solid colour or a gradient, and save the colours you use most.

3D preview

See your design on the rider model before it goes in the game, and check the exact final render.

Pack & install

One click packs a game-ready .pnt paint into your MX Bikes rider folder. You can also export the TGAs.

How it works

Three steps.

Open a template

Load a rider gear PSD from your favourite creator.

Make it yours

Colourways, sponsors, your name and number, placed exactly where you want them.

Ride it

Pack & install, restart MX Bikes, and your gear is in the paint list.

Free & fair

Free to use

Every feature, no limits, no account. Dirt Gear only contacts GitHub to check for updates, and you can switch that off.

Use templates fairly

Only use PSD templates you've bought or been given permission to use. Dirt Graphic Studios doesn't support or endorse using templates that weren't legitimately obtained.

Templates stay with you

Portable projects never include the creator's PSD. Whoever opens one needs their own copy of the template.
